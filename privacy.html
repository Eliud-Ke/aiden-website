01 Overview

Aiden Assistant ("AIDEN", "we", "our", or "the application") is a native Windows desktop artificial intelligence companion designed and operated by Eliud Ocholla. AIDEN operates directly within your personal computer's local operating system environment.

We are committed to absolute data privacy and transparency. Because AIDEN runs locally on your machine, your personal files, credentials, and integration data remain under your direct physical custody. This Privacy Policy details how data is handled when you use AIDEN and its integrations, including Google Calendar.

02 Google User Data & Calendar Integration

AIDEN provides an optional Google Calendar integration allowing you to inspect, schedule, and manage calendar events through natural conversational dialogue.

When you explicitly connect your Google Account, AIDEN requests access to:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events: View, create, update, and delete calendar events strictly when you ask AIDEN to schedule, reschedule, or clear events.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly: View your calendar agenda, event titles, times, and attendees to render interactive schedule cards and detect double-booking conflicts.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email: Display your connected Google identity within the application's account settings.

Google API Limited Use Disclosure

Aiden Assistant's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements.

03 Local Device Storage & Encryption

AIDEN does not operate an external database or cloud synchronization server for your personal data.

  • OAuth Tokens: Your Google OAuth refresh tokens are saved strictly on your local PC inside Windows Credential Manager using the operating system's native Data Protection API (DPAPI). Tokens are never written to plain-text files or transmitted to any third party.
  • Calendar Data: Calendar information is fetched dynamically via direct HTTPS requests to Google's official API endpoints and held transiently in your local PC's RAM to answer your immediate prompt.
  • Episodic & Semantic Memory: Long-term user memories and preferences are stored exclusively on your local storage drive under %LOCALAPPDATA%\AidenAssistant.

04 No Sale, Sharing, or Secondary Use of Data

We firmly believe your personal life and schedule belong to you alone.

  • We NEVER sell, rent, lease, or monetize your Google user data or personal information.
  • We NEVER share your calendar data or personal context with any third-party advertisers, data brokers, or marketing entities.
  • We NEVER use your Google user data to train generalized AI models.
  • Data retrieved from Google APIs is used solely to provide user-facing scheduling features initiated by your commands.

05 User Control, Revocation, and Data Deletion

You retain complete control over your accounts and credentials at all times:

  • In-App Disconnect: You can disconnect your Google Calendar at any time from AIDEN's Settings window. Disconnecting immediately purges your OAuth credentials from Windows Credential Manager.
  • Google Account Permissions: You can revoke AIDEN's access remotely at any time by visiting your Google Account Security Dashboard.
  • Complete Reset: Deleting the %LOCALAPPDATA%\AidenAssistant directory completely wipes all local memory and history from your computer.

06 Security Measures

All network communication between AIDEN and Google's servers occurs strictly over encrypted TLS 1.3/HTTPS channels. Sensitive tokens are isolated in OS-level vaults to protect against unauthorized extraction.
